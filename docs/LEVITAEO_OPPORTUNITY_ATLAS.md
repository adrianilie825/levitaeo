# Levitaeo Opportunity Atlas

*The long-term editorial growth strategy of Levitaeo.*

This document is not technical documentation. It is not an SEO playbook. It is an internal atlas of editorial opportunity — a map of what Levitaeo may publish, and why.

---

## Mission

Levitaeo grows by publishing exceptional editorial collections.

Every new collection must satisfy three conditions:

1. **People search for it.** There is genuine discovery demand — the world is already looking for something Levitaeo can answer with quality.
2. **It has artistic value.** The subject deserves to exist as art, not as content filler.
3. **Competition is reasonable.** The space is not so saturated that only volume wins. There is room for taste.

We do not grow by publishing more. We grow by publishing what matters.

---

## Editorial Opportunity Framework

Every opportunity is evaluated before it becomes an Editorial Project.

### Evaluation Criteria

Each criterion is scored from **1 to 10**.

| Criterion | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| **SEO Demand** | Do people search for this — consistently, internationally? |
| **Competition** | Is the visual field crowded with low-quality or generic work? (10 = open field) |
| **Artistic Potential** | Can Levitaeo produce work that feels like art, not stock? |
| **Editorial Fit** | Does it belong to the Levitaeo house — calm, curated, collectible? |
| **Commercial Potential** | Will collectors acquire editions in this territory? |
| **Evergreen Value** | Will this feel relevant in five years — not tied to a trend? |
| **International Appeal** | Does it translate across cultures without local-only interest? |

**Maximum score: 70**

**Threshold: 55** — Only opportunities scoring above 55 become Editorial Projects.

Scores below 55 may be revisited. They are not rejected forever — they are not ready now.

## 3-Year Editorial Roadmap

### Year 1 — Foundation and proof of taste

Year one establishes Levitaeo as a house of calm discovery rather than volume. Priority goes to **Cities** (hidden-gem skylines), **Nature**, and **Minimalism**: subjects with clear search intent, strong artistic legibility, and room to differentiate through restraint. Launch two to three flagship series—Nordic Harbour Light, Granite Spire, Solitary Form—and use them to train production rhythm, metadata discipline, and collector messaging. Landmarks and Travel enter selectively where scores exceed 55 and production can be executed without compromise.

### Year 2 — Depth and collection coherence

Year two widens the atlas while deepening existing series. **Architecture**, **Botanical**, **Light**, and **Interiors** become primary growth lanes: higher difficulty work that rewards repeat collectors and interior-specifier attention. Cross-link editions within Editorial Series so Skylines, Nature, and Minimal feel like one library. Introduce **Abstract** and **Textures** as Originals-forward experiments only where competition scores remain high. Pause any territory that cannot maintain the 55 threshold at planned quality.

### Year 3 — Maturity and international resonance

Year three optimises for evergreen catalogue value and international appeal. **Geometry**, **Shadows**, and **Coastlines** mature as signature Levitaeo territories—graphic, timeless, and less trend-exposed. Re-score the long tail; promote dormant opportunities only if search and competition have improved. Year three is not expansion for its own sake: it is curation of a coherent collector journey from first discovery print to room-scale statement work.


## Editorial Decision Rules

1. **Score first, sentiment second.** An opportunity must exceed **55/70** before resources are assigned. Enthusiasm does not override the framework.
2. **One series, one promise.** Each Editorial Series must have a identifiable visual covenant; if a subject breaks that covenant, it belongs elsewhere or not at all.
3. **Hidden gems over exhausted icons.** Prefer underrepresented places and angles; when an icon is unavoidable, require a demonstrably fresh composition or detail discipline.
4. **Art before traffic.** SEO potential opens the door; artistic potential determines whether Levitaeo walks through it.
5. **Edition dignity.** No opportunistic drops tied to fleeting news cycles unless the work will read as art in five years.
6. **Collection integrity.** Every release must map cleanly to Originals, Skylines, Nature, Minimal, Architecture, or Botanical—or the mapping must be intentional and explained internally.
7. **Revisit, do not hoard.** Scores below 55 are deferred, not deleted; the atlas is living, but production slots are finite.
8. **Stop when crowded.** If competition collapses toward generic stock, pause the territory even if search volume rises.

*Publish less. Publish what endures.*

Levitaeo is not built on urgency. It is built on works people keep looking at—on walls, in memory, and in search—long after the moment has passed. This atlas is the map; restraint is the compass.
